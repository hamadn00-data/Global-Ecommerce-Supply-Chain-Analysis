# 🌍 Global Ecommerce & Supply Chain Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics portfolio project** analyzing ecommerce sales, customer behavior, marketing performance, product returns, inventory levels, and supplier risk.

The project demonstrates how **SQL, Python, and Power BI** can be used together to transform raw business data into actionable insights and recommendations.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to answer key business questions across the ecommerce and supply chain operations:

* Which products and categories generate the most revenue and profit?
* Which customers contribute the most revenue?
* How do premium and non-premium customers compare?
* Which marketing channels generate the strongest return on investment?
* Which products and categories have high return rates?
* Which products are below their reorder points?
* Which suppliers present potential supply-chain risks?
* How do revenue, profit, and sales performance change over time?

The analysis covers **100,000+ ecommerce transactions**, 8,000 customers, 500 products, inventory data, supplier data, marketing data, returns, and historical pricing.

## 📁 Dataset

The project uses multiple datasets representing different parts of an ecommerce business:

* **Transactions** — sales, revenue, profit, discounts, channels, and order status
* **Customers** — customer demographics, registration, and premium status
* **Products** — product, category, brand, pricing, and cost information
* **Inventory** — stock levels, reorder points, warehouses, and supplier lead times
* **Returns** — return reasons and refund amounts
* **Price History** — pricing, competitor prices, promotions, sales, and margins
* **Marketing Spend** — channel spending, orders, customers, revenue, ROAS, and CAC
* **Supplier Costs** — supplier pricing, lead times, reliability, and ordering information

> Raw datasets are not included in this repository due to file-size considerations.

## 🔎 Key Analytical Areas

### 💰 Sales & Profitability

Analyzed revenue, profit, profit margins, sales channels, categories, and top-performing products to identify the major drivers of ecommerce performance.

### 👥 Customer Analytics

Evaluated customer revenue, order behavior, premium membership, country-level performance, and high-value customers.

### 📢 Marketing Analytics

Compared marketing investment with generated revenue and calculated **ROAS** and customer acquisition metrics to identify more efficient channels.

### 🔄 Returns Analysis

Measured return rates by category and identified products that combine **high revenue with high return rates**, highlighting potential areas for product or quality investigation.

### 📦 Inventory & Supply Chain

Identified products below their reorder points and combined inventory information with supplier lead times and reliability scores to highlight potential supply-chain risks.
